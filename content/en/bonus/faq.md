# Bonus

## FAQ

**Q:** Will there be a sequel?\
**A:** Yes, I have 3 volumes planned.
Volume 2: Mithrendain Heroes.
Volume 3: Shadow Wanderer.
Volume 4: A Dance of Shadows (Remastered).

Mithrendain Heroes will be a collection of short chronicles presenting the early adventures of Cecil, Lathai, and Aranira. This will follow up on Shadow Wanderer, which will narrate Cecil's unholy crusade that is mentioned in Chapter 8, Episode 3 of Volume 1.
Then, with all the experience I will have acquired, I will remaster the first novel.

###

**Q:** Will there be an Ebook version?\
**A:** No. Ebooks are too hard to produce, distribute, maintain, and update. Unfortunately, that's not something I want to spend my time on.

###

**Q:** I found your novel somewhere other than on this site. Is it you?\
**A:** No. [https://v1.feybound.fr](https://v1.feybound.fr) is the only and official source.

###

**Q:** What is the publication date of the novel?\
**A:** June 13, 2024.
