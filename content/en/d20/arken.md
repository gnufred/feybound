## Race Features: {{<dnd "Warforged" "sources/erftlw">}}
**Age:** The maximum Warforged lifespan remains a mystery. You are immune to magical aging effects.

**Constructed Resilience:**
- Advantage on saving throws against being poisoned.
- Resistance to poison damage.
- You don't need to eat, drink, or breathe.
- You are immune to disease.
- You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep.

**Sentry's Rest:** To rest, you must spend six hours in an inactive, motionless state. In this state, you appear inert, but it doesn't render you unconscious, and you can see and hear as normal.

**Integrated Protection:** You gain a +1 bonus to Armor Class.

**Specialized Design:** Gain an additional skilll profeciency.

## Class Features: {{<dnd "Clockwork Soul" "sources/tcoe">}}
**Restore Balance:** Use your reaction to cause a creature to roll with advantage or disadvantage.

**Quickened Spell:** Cast a spell as a bonus action instead of an action.

**Extended Spell:** Extend the duration of a spell to 24 hours.

**Empowered Spell:** Reroll damage on a spell.

**Magical Guidance:** Spend a Sorcery Point to reroll a failed ability check.

**Bastion of Law:** As an action, expend Sorcery Points to ward yourself or a creature.
