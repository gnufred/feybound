# Temporal Rift
*6th-level conjuration*

**Casting Time:** 8 hours

**Range:** Self (up to 4 willing creatures within 10 feet)

**Components:** V, S, M (a crystal hourglass with diamond dust worth at least 500 gp, which the spell consumes)

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Description:**

By manipulating the threads of time and space, the spellcaster creates a temporal rift that transports the caster and up to four willing creatures to a different plane of existence. The caster must concentrate deeply, focusing on the intended destination while using the crystal hourglass to measure the passage of time. As the spell reaches its culmination, the hourglass dissolves into a shimmering vortex of temporal energy, enveloping the caster and their companions. The swirling energy warps the fabric of reality around them, pulling them through the temporal rift to their desired plane.

For the spell duration, as an action, the spellcaster can reconstruct the hourglass and revert it, instantly teleporting all transported creatures to their positions when the spell was first cast.
