## Subclass Features: Season Wanderer
*Cecil's subclass is based on the {{<dnd "Fey Wanderer" "sources/tcoe">}} subclass, but includes custom changes.*

**Rapid Seasonal Change:**\
*3rd-level Season Wanderer feature*

Due to an extensive mix of physical, natural, breath work, and harsh weather exposure, Cecil can easily change the season to which he is attuned.
- Change your Eladrin season as a bonus action.
- Uses per proficiency bonus per long rest.

**Elemental Strikes:**\
*3rd-level Season Wanderer feature*

Cecil's mastery over the seasons allows him to channel the raw elemental forces of nature into his attacks, infusing each strike with the power of his current seasonal attunement.

Once per turn, based on your attuned season, deal increased elemental damage on a successful attack.
- ~~**Autumn:** Earth~~.
- **Winter:** Cold.
- **Spring:** Lightning.
- **Summer:** Fire.

**Otherworldly Glamour:**\
*3rd-level Fey Wanderer feature*

Add Wisdom modifier to Charisma checks. Proficiency in Persuasion.

**Fey Wanderer Magic:**\
*3rd-level Fey Wanderer feature*

You gain additional spells at specific levels.

**Seasonal Twist:**\
*7th-level Season Wanderer feature*

Cecil can take advantage of failed enemy manipulations and turn their power into the fury of the seasons.

Advantage on saving throws against charmed or frightened. On success, use your reaction to trigger an effect based on your season.
- ~~**Autumn:** Charm one creature~~.
- **Winter:** Frighten one creature.
- **Spring:** Teleport a creature 30 feet away from its position.
- **Summer:** Deal fire damage to one creature.

## Unique Features

**Autumn's Death:**

Due to a past experience, Cecil has permanently lost the ability to attune to the season of Autumn.

**Shadow-Infused Eyes:**

Cecil’s eyes possess an eerie transformation, with dark, almost black sclera and irises that shimmer like liquid shadows. These eyes, a manifestation of his curse and connection to the Shadow Lord, allow Cecil to peer deeply into the intentions of others.

The dark magic imbued in his eyes reveals the hidden truths and underlying intentions that most would miss, making him an exceptional judge of character and intent.

- Skill proficiency: Insight.

**Veil of Authority:**

The Shadow Lord's touch bestows Cecil with a supernatural aura of confidence and charisma, enveloping him in an enigmatic presence that commands attention and respect. This otherworldly authority enhances his natural charm, making him irresistibly attractive and compelling to those around him.
- +4 Charisma.

## Class Features: Ranger
**Favored Foe:** For one minute, deal increased damage to a marked creature.

**Fighting Style: Archery:** +2 bonus to attack rolls with ranged weapons.

**Extra Attack:** You can attack twice, when you take the Attack action.

**Roving:** Walk speed +5 feet. Climb and swim speed = walk speed.

**Land's Stride:** Movement unhampered by nonmagical terrain. Advantage on saving throws against magically created plants that impede movement.

**Tireless:** As an action, gain temporary hit points. After a short rest, reduce exhaustion level by 1.
