# Timeless Staff
*Staff, legendary (requires attunement by a sorcerer)*

This staff, carved from ancient wood and inlaid with intricate runes that shimmer with temporal magic, is a rare and powerful artifact. Its wielder can manipulate the fabric of time itself, gaining unparalleled control over their magical abilities.

**Armor Class:** +1 bonus to AC while holding this staff.

**Saving Throws:** +2 bonus to all saving throws while attuned to this staff.

**Sorcery Points:** The number of sorcery points available to you is doubled while attuned to this staff.

## Additional Properties:
**Requires Attunement:** You must be a sorcerer to attune to the Timeless Staff.
