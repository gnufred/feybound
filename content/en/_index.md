## Summary

In the mystical realms where light and shadow intertwine, "Feybound: A Dance of Shadows" unfolds a tale of ethereal beauty and perilous darkness. Cecil, an {{<dnd "Eladrin" "sources/mtof">}} ranger marked by the enigmatic Shadow Lord, must navigate the delicate balance between his light and dark natures. His shadow-infused eyes, a curse and a blessing, reveal hidden truths and cast a haunting allure. By his side stands Elara Brightmoon, a devoted priestess of {{<dnd "Sehanine Moonbow" "sources/scag">}}, the lunar goddess of dreams, torn between her sacred duties and her deep, unspoken love for Cecil. Together, they embark on a journey through the Feywild and beyond, seeking to restore the balance that the shadow threatens to unravel.

Accompanied by Arken, a {{<dnd "Warforged" "sources/erftlw">}} sorcerer with the ability to manipulate time, their quest leads them to ancient temples, forbidden forests, and the heart of arcane mysteries. As they confront dark fey creatures, unravel celestial secrets, and face their own inner demons, the trio's bond is tested and strengthened. "Feybound: A Dance of Shadows" weaves a mesmerizing narrative of love, duty, and the eternal struggle between light and dark. This epic adventure promises to captivate readers with its richly detailed world, complex characters, and the timeless allure of the {{<dnd "Feywild" "sources/mtof">}}.

## About the Author

**Frédéric Potvin** is an accomplished Internet Engineer with over two decades of experience in the field.

Beyond his professional endeavors, Frédéric has made significant contributions as a volunteer, having participated in numerous Red Cross rescue missions. His commitment to community service reflects his dedication and compassionate nature.

An avid enthusiast of Dungeons & Dragons, Frédéric has been immersed in the game since the release of the first 3rd edition rulebooks. This passion for D&D has profoundly influenced his creativity and storytelling abilities.

In his personal life, Frédéric is a proud father of two, an avid runner who has covered thousands of kilometers, and a music enthusiast with a vast collection of HiFi music albums. He also enjoys playing guitar around campfires, with a repertoire of many songs.
