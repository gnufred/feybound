# Epilogue

## The Ascension

In the heart of the celestial temple dedicated to {{<dnd "Sehanine Moonbow" "sources/scag">}}, Elara Brightmoon stood in the interior garden, surrounded by acolytes. The garden, a place of serene beauty, was filled with the delicate scent of night-blooming flowers. Moonlight filtered through the canopy of the garden trees, casting a white glow over the sacred space.

There were reunited for a miraculous event. One that few could ever hope to withness. The acolytes chanted loudly, their complex prayers echoing through the garden. Their voices, filled with knowledge of the transformation to come, harmonized with the bright moonlight.

Elara stood at the center of the garden, her silken robes glimmering in the moonlight. She stood calmly, her heart filled with both serenity and determination. She raised her hands towards the sky, closed her eyes and began her prayer.

"{{<dnd "Sehanine Moonbow" "sources/scag">}}, goddess of the moon and dreams, hear my plea. I stand before you, a humble servant who has been tested by the shadows and the light. My journey has been fraught with challenges, from the seductive lure of forbidden love to the harrowing trials of my mission.

In the depths of the Shadow Realm, I confronted and defeated the Shadow Lord and his Chosen. Through this long journey, I have felt the constant weight of Cecil’s love and passion. Though my heart wavered for him, my faith in you remained unbroken.

{{<dnd "Sehanine" "sources/scag">}}, I offer my soul to you, seeking to transcend the sorrows and conflicts that have marked my path. Cleanse me of the burdens that weigh upon my spirit, and let your divine grace fill me with purpose. Transform my experiences into wisdom, my trials into strength, so that I may become a beacon of hope and guidance for others.

Guide me as I leave behind the shadows and rise into your celestial embrace. Let your light shine through me, illuminating the path for those who are lost and in pain. May I forever walk in your serene glow, a symbol of resilience and grace, devoted to your divine will.

{{<dnd "Sehanine Moonbow" "sources/scag">}}, accept my offering. Let your divine power flow through me, transforming my spirit into an eternal reflection of your beauty and compassion. May your dreams guide my steps, and your light be my eternal companion."

As Elara's words filled the air, her body began to glow. The light of the moon enveloped her in a viel of divine magic, and she felt the comforting, powerful presence of her goddess merging with her essence. The lingering power of the Shadow Lord within her intertwined with the moonlight, creating a complex and potent blend of energies.

The fusion of moonlight and shadow energy created a swirling vortex of luminescent and dark tendrils around Elara. The garden's ambient magic amplified the transformation, causing the flowers and leaves to shimmer in response to the celestial power. Elara's form began to change, her physical appearance shifting to reflect the merging of divine forces. Her skin took on a luminescent glow, shimmering with an otherworldly light. Her eyes, deep green and filled with wisdom, now sparkled with a mix of moonlight and shadow. Her hair, once dark and short, lengthened and became a flowing cascade of silvery strands interwoven with deep black, reaching down to the ground.

Her garments transformed into a gown woven from the very essence of moonlight and shadow. The fabric shimmered and flowed like liquid, reflecting the interplay of light and dark. Her presence became both fearsome and awe-inspiring, embodying the duality of her new power. As the transformation completed, Elara stood powerfull and radiant, a beacon of divine power. She had become a new form of deity, her essence a perfect fusion of {{<dnd "Sehanine Moonbow's" "sources/scag">}} celestial light and the Shadow Lord's enigmatic darkness.

The acolytes, now imbued with her newfound energy, sang praises in testament to her ascension. Suddenly, the garden's light intensified, and the divine figure of {{<dnd "Sehanine Moonbow" "sources/scag">}} appeared before Elara, her presence filling the space with a powerful but serene glow.

"Elara, my beloved," {{<dnd "Sehanine" "sources/scag">}} spoke, her voice a melodic harmony of the stars, "you have transcended your mortal bounds and embraced your divine destiny. From this day forth, you shall be known as Elara Moonbow, a goddess of moonlight and shadows. Come, join me in the celestial realm as my daughter, and together we will guide and watch over the {{<dnd "Feywild" "sources/mtof">}} and its children, who are scattered across the multiverse." With {{<dnd "Sehanine's" "sources/scag">}} blessing, Elara felt a profound sense of peace and purpose. She accepted her new name and role with grace, feeling the divine connection strengthen within her.

As her first divine act, Elara exhaled sharply, summoning a holy wind that swept across the Shadow Realm. The wind cleared away the gloom of the forest, and the oppressive clouds, revealing an ever present radiant full moon.

Her voice, imbued with the celestial pain and eternal love she bore for Cecil, echoed through the realms. In her silent plea, a divine whisper, indistinct yet profound, wove through the heavens. An aurora borealis unfurled across the sky, shimmering in hues of sorrow and love. As she cried, a celestial rain began, stars falling gently through the night. Each luminous drop a tear of divine sadness, populating the newly unveiled sky with a myriad of radiant, sorrowful stars.
