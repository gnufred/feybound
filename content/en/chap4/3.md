# Ethereal Visions

## Abomination

As the group stood in the misty, eerie expanse of the {{<dnd "Ethereal Plane" "sources/dmg">}}, the air grew dense under the influence of a supernatural presence. Tendrils of darkness twisted and writhed through the fog, distorting the very fabric of reality around them. From the depths of the darkness, a monstrosity emerged, floating sinisterly above the ground. Its form was a grotesque amalgamation of a {{<dnd "beholder" "sources/mm">}} and a {{<dnd "mind flayer" "sources/mm">}}, an abomination born from the darkest corners of the multiverse.

The creature's head was vast and globular, covered in eyes and crowned with a multitude of eyestalks, each ending in a gleaming, sinister eye that moved with malevolent intent. Below its spherical form extended a mass of writhing tentacles, coated in a viscous, translucent film that revealed pulsing veins. The creature's skin was a sickly, semi-transparent membrane through which the grotesque pulsations of its internal organs were visible.

The central eye, larger and more menacing than the others, fixed its gaze on the adventurers, emitting an aura of palpable terror. As it stared at them, the air around it seemed to distort, the ambient magic of the {{<dnd "Ethereal Plane" "sources/dmg">}} warping under its influence. The environment itself seemed to react to the monstrosity's presence: the mists thickened and darkened, while the ground beneath their feet trembled as if recoiling in fear.

The creature's tentacles twisted menacingly, seeming to grasp at the very air. A faint telepathic hum emanated from its form, a sinister whisper that wormed its way into the minds of Cecil, Elara, and Arken, filling them with a sense of foreboding. The monstrosity's eyes glowed with a supernatural light, casting eerie shadows that danced and flickered around the ethereal landscape, turning the scene into a nightmarish vision from another world.

Arken was the first to react, his mind calculating with unprecedented precision the best course of action. Channeling the arcane energies within him, he cast a spell that increased Cecil's speed and agility, making his movements swift and fluid like the wind. As Cecil felt this energy surge through him, Arken quickly followed with a blazing firebolt, aiming to distract the monstrosity.

Cecil, now imbued with supernatural speed, moved with agility, dodging the creature's tentacles. Arken's firebolt struck the creature squarely, its tentacles writhing in pain and anger. The intense light of the spell briefly illuminated the scene, casting dancing shadows that accentuated the nightmarish nature of the confrontation.

The creature's response was immediate and brutal. One of its many eyes focused intensely on Arken, and a beam of green energy shot forth. The disintegration ray struck Arken with devastating force, vaporizing one of his arms and leaving a gaping hole in his metallic body. Despite the excruciating pain and severe damage, Arken's will remained unshaken. He maintained his concentration on the spell that enhanced Cecil's abilities, ensuring that his ally could fight with increased speed and strength.

Feeling the arcane energy of Arken's spell coursing through his being, Cecil tapped into his innate connection to the elements. His body harmonized with the fiery essence of summer, his skin taking on a faint reddish hue and his hair streaking with flaming colors. The haste spell amplified his movements, making him a blur of speed.

With two short swords in hand, Cecil sprang into action. His speed and agility were unmatched, allowing him to dance around the monstrosity with deadly precision. He aimed for the creature's many eyes and tentacles, each strike intended to neutralize and mutilate. The blades of his swords glinted in the eerie light as they sliced through the air, severing eyestalks and tentacles in rapid succession.

As Cecil continued his relentless assault, he utilized his fey teleportation abilities to gain additional tactical advantage. The bursts of flame accompanying his teleportations scorched the monstrosity, causing it to writhe in pain. The sudden shifts in position allowed Cecil to strike more vulnerable points, slicing through its flesh with increased efficiency.

The creature recoiled under the assault, its dismembered parts writhing in agony. Cecil's movements were a blur, his strikes landing with such speed and ferocity that the monstrosity struggled to keep up. The spell had transformed him into a whirlwind of steel and fire, each strike a testament to the enhanced speed and strength granted by Arken's magic. His fiery teleportations added to the chaos, setting parts of the creature aflame and creating openings for further attacks.

As Cecil maintained his relentless assault, Elara began to regain her composure. Recovering from the disorienting effects of her previous ordeal, she focused on the ongoing battle. Channeling her divine energy, she cast a powerful spell, summoning a swarm of ethereal insects. These otherworldly locusts, shimmering with a supernatural light, filled the air around the monstrosity.

The insects swarmed the creature, biting and stinging with relentless fury. The monstrosity writhed in confusion and pain as the insects attacked from all sides. Their constant buzzing and piercing bites disrupted its concentration, causing it to thrash wildly in a desperate attempt to fend them off.

In a desperate counterattack, the creature unleashed a devastating explosion, targeting Elara. The psychic wave struck her with immense force, violently throwing her backward and rendering her unconscious. As she fell, the swarm of insects began to dissipate, the spell's effect waning with her lost concentration.

The monstrosity, now gravely wounded and suffering immensely, sought to escape the onslaught. In a final desperate effort, it invoked its innate ability to plane shift. The air around it shimmered and distorted as it vanished from the battlefield, retreating to the beyond to recover.

The battle ended as abruptly as it had begun. Cecil, still energized by Arken's spell, rushed to Elara's side, his heart pounding with a mix of adrenaline and concern.

Knowing it was time to leave, Arken, with his remaining arm, began to channel the arcane energy of the temporal rift. His runes glowed intensely as he focused on reconstructing the broken hourglass. The shards of the hourglass floated in the air, drawn together by his powerful magic. As they reassembled, the hourglass filled again with magical diamond dust, shimmering with the essence of time itself.

Grasping the hourglass, Arken turned it over, the sand inside shimmering and mingling with the ambient arcane energy. The air around them began to warp and twist, reality itself bending under the pressure of the powerful spell. The very fabric of the plane rippled and crackled with arcane energy as the magic of the hourglass took effect.

The group felt the intense pull of the spell, their surroundings transforming into a whirlwind of colors and light. The sensation was both exhilarating and overwhelming, as if they were being swept away in a storm of pure magic. The plane seemed to resist their departure, but the power of the hourglass was undeniable, opening a path through the chaotic energy.

In a final blinding flash of light, the group was torn from the {{<dnd "Ethereal Plane" "sources/dmg">}}, the portal closing behind them. The transition was intense, leaving them momentarily disoriented as they found themselves back in the familiar surroundings from which they had come.
