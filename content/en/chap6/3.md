# Resurrection

## Seeing the Light

Elara, her heart heavy with both hope and despair, lifted a golden cup filled with crushed diamond powder. Tears streamed down her face, each drop a testament to her unspoken love and unwavering faith. She allowed her tears to fall into the cup, mingling with the diamond dust, their combined essence shimmering with a faint magical light.

At that moment, Liora stepped forward, her voice rising above the haunting melodies of the shadow choir. Channeling all the power of the assembled voices, she infused the tears with reverberations of their collective chant, her magical voice weaving a spell of unity and strength.

{{<dnd "Sehanine Moonbow" "sources/scag">}}, goddess of the moon and dreams, heard their prayer. From the heavens, she sent spiritual creatures, each connected to the radiant light of the moon. Moon moths, pixies shimmering in moonlight, and ghostly owls descended into the clearing, their presence adding to the growing power of the ritual. They joined Elara's choir, their voices blending harmoniously into the final prayer.

The moonlight intensified, its beams piercing the shadows. The light grew stronger, pushing back the darkness and causing the shadowy specters to waver and vanish. The radiant glow bathed Cecil's body, beginning to reverse the curse that had tormented him.

Cecil's eyes, once infused with darkness, began to glow with a soft silver light. The transformation spread through his body, the moonlight purifying the darkness from his soul. Slowly, his eyes opened, revealing the complete change within him. The divine light was so powerful it overcame the spectral incarnation of the Shadow Lord, who vanished in the presence of {{<dnd "Sehanine's" "sources/scag">}} blessing.

The faerie choir's song reached its climax, with the harmonious blend of moonlit voices and divine power filling the forest with an awe-inspiring light. Cecil, now bathed in the goddess's grace, took his first breath, the curse lifted and his soul restored.
