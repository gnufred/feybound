During the initial drafting of the novel, I concluded at the end of chapter 8, with the confrontation ending unexpectedly.

At that moment, I still didn't know how to conclude the story. I had several ideas and had roughly written the epilogue parts 1 and 2 in their current form. Part 3 was still missing, which you have just read.

I had to let my ideas simmer for several days and turn the story over and over before arriving at this idea. I didn't want my story to end well or poorly. And I didn't want it to open up to a sequel either.

When I finally had the idea of the Legendary Song, I started to cry. Immediately, I returned to the keyboard and rewrote the two drafts and this part. I cried non-stop during the writing. I could no longer see through my fogged glasses.

I am extremely satisfied with it. I believe it is touching and original.
